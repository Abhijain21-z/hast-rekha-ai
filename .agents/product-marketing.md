# Hast Rekha AI — Product Marketing Context

Updated: 24 October 2026

## What it is

Hast Rekha AI is a free, instant Vedic astrology + palmistry reading website for Indian users. A visitor enters birth details (and optionally a palm photo) and gets a bilingual report with rashi, nakshatra, lagna, 12 life-area predictions, palm-line analysis, lucky years and remedies. A paid ₹51 Pro report adds a five-year roadmap, life-partner indications and detailed remedies; a ₹351 Ultra Pro plan adds the book.

## Core promise

"Apni hatheli aur kundli se apna agla saal samjho — free, instant, Hindi aur English mein."

## Audience

- Primary: Hindi-first mobile users in India, 18–45, curious about palmistry, rashifal, marriage, career and money.
- Secondary: English-comfortable Indian readers and diaspora users.
- High-intent moments: Diwali/New Year, job change, marriage timing, money problems, relationship confusion.

## Differentiators

- Free reading with no login/card required.
- Rule-based Vedic calculations, not a black-box paid AI API.
- Palm lines + kundli in one report, not either/or.
- Full Hindi and English content.
- Transparent pricing: ₹51 Pro, ₹351 Ultra Pro.

## Primary conversion

Start a free reading → view the report → unlock ₹51 Pro for the five-year roadmap, partner indications and remedies.

## Brand voice

Warm, direct, culturally fluent Hindi/Hinglish. No fear-selling, no guaranteed outcomes, no miracle claims. Always include the entertainment/self-reflection disclaimer.

## Measurement

- Google Search Console: impressions, clicks, CTR, indexing.
- Vercel Analytics: real-time visitors, top pages, referrers, countries.
- UTM convention: `utm_source` (whatsapp/telegram/x/facebook/copy), `utm_medium` (social/referral), `utm_campaign` (share).

## Constraints

- Do not publish or submit to directories without owner approval.
- Do not promise traffic or financial outcomes.
- Keep Cashfree payment/webhook changes owner-verified.
- Every new indexable page needs a unique title, description, canonical, one H1 and internal links.
